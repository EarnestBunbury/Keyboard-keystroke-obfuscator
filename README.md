# RP2040 Keystroke Obfuscator

Have tape over your monitor's camera?  Well, this is for your keyboard; to prevent AI from tracking your keyboard speed and movements, which it uses to find your AI profile.  It only takes a few seconds before using your typing method and cadence to know who you are.  
Also, buy me a coffee.  I'm not jittery enough.  Link at the bottom.

Stops AI and behavioral tracking software from profiling you through your typing cadence, rhythm, and keystroke patterns. Intercepts raw input, holds it in a local RAM buffer, and outputs text via synthetic, randomized bursts so local telemetry scripts can't fingerprint your writing style or map your keystrokes.

## How It Works

Sits as a hardware intermediary between your keyboard and your machine, injecting unpredictable micro-delays and smoothing out typing metrics so automated behavioral analysis tools get garbage data instead of a biometric signature.
